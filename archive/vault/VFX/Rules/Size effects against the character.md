# Size effects against the character

> TL;DR
> - Size every beat in character heights (H, about 5 studs) and look at it beside a character.
> - Attacks must clearly out-size the body that makes or takes them: arcs 2-3 H, a band thicker than a limb.
> - A hit spills past the target's silhouette on every side.
> - Reference auras put a 1.4-3.4 H ground layer under a 1.2-1.3 H body envelope.

## Evidence
A 1.3 H slash with a thin band was "a bit small; unclear what it is meant for"; the same combo's 2.5 H finisher read.
