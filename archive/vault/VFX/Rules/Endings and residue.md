# Endings and residue

> TL;DR
> - The break or disappearance is a highlight people remember.
> - Warn (shake or glint), turn white-hot for 2-3 frames, break in one frame into a stump and 3-7 pieces, small things pop out.
> - Breaks travel outward over about 0.15 s so you see many separate pops.
> - Residue (marks, frost, scorch) holds at 90%+ until about 80% of the linger, then fades softly.

## Evidence
Owner's favourite moment was "when the ice crystals exploded"; 0.1.6 removed its frost 0.5 s too early.
