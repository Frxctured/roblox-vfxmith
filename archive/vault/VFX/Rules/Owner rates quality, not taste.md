# Owner rates quality, not taste

> TL;DR
> - The goal is matching professional, human-made quality.
> - Taste belongs to each user; the owner's taste is versatile, and he rates how close something is to pro work.
> - 10 = professional, expensive work; a casual viewer's 9 was the owner's 7.
> - Acceptance needs both: beating every earlier version and passing his side-by-side review against pro effects.

## Applies to
Rating pages, benchmark rounds and any judge trained on his labels.
