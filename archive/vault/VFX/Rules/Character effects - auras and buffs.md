# Character effects - auras and buffs

> TL;DR
> - Most of an aura is on the ground: the ground layer is the biggest element, 1.4-3.4 H wide, feet in its glow.
> - One small accent at the head in a hue shifted 30-50 degrees; the face stays clear.
> - Asymmetry: one tall accent or a second mass to one side; perfect symmetry reads static.
> - Soft layers behind the body, hard bright ones in front; big structure holds still, life is in textures and particles.

## Rhythm
Heartbeats switch on in 1 frame and decay over 0.4-0.8 s every 0.7-1.1 s with jitter; several unsynced periods so no
loop shows.
