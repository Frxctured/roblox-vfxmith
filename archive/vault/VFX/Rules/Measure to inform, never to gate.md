# Measure to inform, never to gate

> TL;DR
> - Creative work isn't bound to fixed rules: a planted foot may slide a lot, depending on the animation.
> - Numbers inform judgement and describe a result; they never pass or fail a creative choice.
> - Owner sketches and examples are starting points to tune, not exact specs.

## Applies to
Stats like [[Our effects look matte - Brightness and LightEmission vs pros]] and any fault checker: faults are
objective breakages (invisible, clipping, over budget), never style thresholds.
