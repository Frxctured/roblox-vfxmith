# Smoke is a volume

> TL;DR
> - Puffs overlap by a third or more, start fairly opaque (Transparency 0.2-0.4) and vary about 3x in size.
> - Two tones: darker core or underside, lighter edge; its value differs from what's behind it.
> - Born on the thing that makes them, moving one way (rise, roll, get pulled) with Drag.
> - Leftover particles mix 3-4 sizes and 2+ shapes, fast at birth, slowed by Drag, cooling white to body to deep.

## Evidence
Eight faint, separate, same-size puffs were "kinda low quality"; a flat white cloud the same value as the pale
character hid the character.
