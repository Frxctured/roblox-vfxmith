vfxmith

Say "make a cool light beam with an explosion" and get an effect built the way experienced Roblox VFX artists build them. vfxmith studied ~1,900 real open-source effects, layer by layer and frame by frame, and turns that into references, recipes and a build loop an AI agent can follow.

How an agent makes an effect

  1. Find references

    find_effects.py "light beam with an explosion" splits the request into parts and returns the closest real effects: exact layer values, frames, the effect-type study.

  2. Start from recipes

    One spec with both recipes' layers, the explosion shifted to the beam's impact, adapted with values from the cards.

  3. Play it in Studio and look

    The kit installs without Rojo or uploads; frozen frames are captured on any OS through the Studio MCP.

  4. Compare and review

    Side by side with the references; three blind reviewer agents (shape, timing, colour) list concrete fixes until the scores stop rising.

Works as a plain folder too: point any agent at it and it starts at AGENTS.md.

What the effects taught us

  1. Fire everything at t = 0. Lifetimes make the timeline: flash 0.015-0.16 s, rings 0.1-1.1 s, smoke 0.35-2.5 s, ground marks 0.5-2.5 s.
  2. Brightness = 0 turns any texture black. Give every bright shape a black twin, a little bigger, slightly behind. No ink = a white blob.
  3. Opaque glow beats additive stacks. 44% of emitters use LightEmission 0 or less; a dark red at Brightness 60 is a white-hot core with a red edge in one layer.
  4. Fade by colour, not transparency. Ramps that end at black fade cleanly; one shared ramp ties the whole effect together.
  5. Pop, then settle. Flashes appear at full size and shrink; rings reach a quarter to half of their size in the first 10% of their life.
  6. Burst and stop. A particle travels about Speed / Drag studs.
  7. Flat rings: VelocityPerpendicular, Speed ~0.005, SpreadAngle 0-15 (flat) or 3333 (a shell of random tilts).
  8. Size everything from one number and set Brightness by screen area: veils 0.05-0.16, rings 1-3, sparks 15-35, embers 50-200.

All of it, with sources and numbers: knowledge/.

The open-source pack, cleaned

The plugin's library comes from a large collection of open-source Roblox VFX shared by their creators. Every one of its 10,103 scripts was decoded without opening Studio and checked. Two files had real backdoors (neutralised), one leaked a webhook token (removed), and five files ripped from live games were left out. The cleaned pack is a separate download; the effects belong to their creators.
