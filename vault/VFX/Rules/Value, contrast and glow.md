# Value, contrast and glow

> TL;DR
> - A dark ink layer sits next to the brightest shape; one hue family from near-black to a white-hot core.
> - Exactly one dominant bright mass; white only at 1-3 focal points, hue-shifted, under about 5% of the frame at the peak.
> - Solid hero shapes don't glow while they hold; flashes are 0.1-0.15 s.
> - One effect light, Range about 1.6x the effect radius, Brightness 3 or less, off within about 0.3 s.
> - Saturation suits the material: natural materials slightly below full, magic and fire higher.

## Evidence
The preferred ice slam changed 21% of the screen at the hit; an over-glowing one 52%, still 49% at 0.5 s.
